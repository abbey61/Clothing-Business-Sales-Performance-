# Clothing-Business-Sales-Performance-
An analysis of a global retail dataset (20k+ rows) using Power BI, and the insights were a masterclass in consumer behavior. While we often think "more ads = more money," the numbers told a different story.
KEY INSIGHTS

- Clothe Business sales data of 20,340 rows, 20,252 products, 5 clothing type distributed between 12 contries.

- China generated the most revenue (19.89%), most product sold(20.04%), while Argentina generated the least revenue (0.85%), with least volume of product sold (0.89%, among the 12 countries.

- products with No promtion generated most revenue with less sales volume than promoted products

- Jacket has the highest revenue generated (55.13%) of the total revenue, while Jeans generated (3.30%) as the lowest revenue generated by type of cloth

- Women cloths had x2 of men cloth promotion

- Women Cloths also generated the hightest revenue, 67.77% of the total revenue., with avg price of $41.89. 

- Most Clothe was sold during the Autumn and Winter Seasons. And women clothe hss x2 of the total men clothe sold

- clothes position on the Aisle of the store were sold more and it generated the most  revenue by store placement
